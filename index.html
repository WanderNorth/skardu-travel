Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan

Skardu, where the Karakoram begins

Alpine lakes, a cold desert, ancient forts and the road to the world's great peaks, all within reach of one valley town.

Skardu Travel See the places

Places to see

Six reasons to stay longer than a night.

Shangrila and Kachura Lakes

Calm lakes ringed by orchards, close to town and good for a first day.

Sarfaranga Cold Desert

Sand dunes set against snow peaks, best at sunrise and sunset.

Deosai National Park

A high plateau of wildflowers and open sky, reached by a long mountain road.

Shigar Fort

A restored historic fort in the Shigar Valley, with quiet courtyards.

Satpara Lake

A large blue lake just outside town, a short and easy outing.

Khaplu Valley

Traditional villages and a palace guesthouse on the road east.

When to go

Check road and weather conditions before you travel.

Spring

Apricot and cherry blossom in the valleys, with cool mornings.

Summer

The main season. High roads such as Deosai are usually open.

Autumn

Golden leaves, clear light and fewer visitors.

A sample four-day route

  1. Day 1: Arrive, Satpara Lake and the old bazaar.
  2. Day 2: Shangrila and the Kachura lakes.
  3. Day 3: Shigar Fort and Sarfaranga Cold Desert.
  4. Day 4: Deosai National Park, then return.
